About

Andhra Spice and Family Restaurant is located in Maria Puram, Kadapa, serving bold Rayalaseema and coastal Andhra cooking — gongura, ulavacharu, natukodi, and dum biryani made the traditional way.

The menu is built around sharing: family packs, combo packs with rotis and curry included, and bucket biryanis meant for a full table rather than one plate. Outdoor catering is available for parties and functions.

Every order is cooked fresh to the ticket — please allow around 20 minutes, and note that once a dish is served or an order placed, it can't be cancelled.

🪣 Bucket Biryani
Chicken, mutton, fish or prawns, dum-cooked and served in a full-size bucket with rotis, curry & roast — built for the whole family.
🍚 Choice of rice
Most biryanis are available with Basmathi rice or local Chitty Muthyalu rice, at the same price.
🎉 Outdoor Catering
Available for parties, functions and events — ask staff for group pricing.
📦 Family & Combo Packs
Biryani, curry and rotis bundled together across chicken, mutton and veg options.
