About[cite: 16]

Andhra Spice and Family Restaurant is located in Maria Puram, Kadapa, serving bold Rayalaseema and coastal Andhra cooking — gongura, ulavacharu, natukodi, and dum biryani made the traditional way.[cite: 16]

The menu is built around sharing: family packs, combo packs with rotis and curry included, and bucket biryanis meant for a full table rather than one plate. Outdoor catering is available for parties and functions.[cite: 16]

Every order is cooked fresh to the ticket — please allow around 20 minutes, and note that once a dish is served or an order placed, it can't be cancelled.[cite: 16]

🪣 Bucket Biryani[cite: 16]
Chicken, mutton, fish or prawns, dum-cooked and served in a full-size bucket with rotis, curry & roast — built for the whole family.[cite: 16]
🍚 Choice of rice[cite: 16]
Most biryanis are available with Basmathi rice or local Chitty Muthyalu rice, at the same price.[cite: 16]
🎉 Outdoor Catering[cite: 16]
Available for parties, functions and events — ask staff for group pricing.[cite: 16]
📦 Family & Combo Packs[cite: 16]
Biryani, curry and rotis bundled together across chicken, mutton and veg options.[cite: 16]
